Questions

Before you ask.

Will a moodie fit my bulb?

Moodies are one-size-fits-most covers for standard CFL and LED bulbs. The silicone stretches to hug the glass.

Is it safe to leave on?

Yes, when it’s on a CFL or LED bulb. Moodies are made from heat-safe silicone for these cooler-running bulbs. Don’t use them on incandescent or halogen bulbs.

How bright is the room with one on?

A moodie tints the light rather than blocking it, so you get a colored glow. Expect a softer, moodier room, which is the whole point.

Can I reuse them?

Over and over. Pull one off, put another on, keep the ones you’re not using in a drawer for the next party.
