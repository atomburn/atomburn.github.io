How it works

Turn any CFL or LED into a mood light.

There’s nothing to install or rewire, and you keep the bulb you already own.

STEP 01

Pick a color

Each Moodie is a soft, heat-safe silicone cap. It stretches to fit and snaps back.

STEP 02

Slip it on

With the light off and cool, pull the cap over the bulb. It holds on by itself.

STEP 03

Switch on

The whole room takes the color. Swap caps whenever you want a different one.

Questions

Before you ask.

What’s in the Rainbow Pack?

Six Moodies in two pouches. The Hot 3-pack has red, pink and orange. The Cool 3-pack has purple, green and blue.

Will a Moodie fit my bulb?

Moodies are made for compact fluorescent (CFL) and LED bulbs. The silicone stretches to hug the bulb.

Is it safe to leave on?

Yes, on a CFL or LED bulb. Moodies are heat-safe silicone made for these cooler-running bulbs. Don’t use them on incandescent bulbs.

Can I reuse them?

Over and over. Pull one off, put another on, and keep the rest in the pouch for the next party.

Do Moodies actually change your mood?

Moodies change the color of your light. Our lawyer would like to point out that the rest is a command, not a claim.
